# antislop audit 001 — 2026-09-26

Scope: the font swap to Montserrat (`resources/css/app.css` `@theme`, the three
`fonts.bunny.net` links, `resources/views/students/rapor.blade.php` inline styles).
Mode: 2 (audit after the fact). Nothing has been fixed beyond the font swap itself.

Reading this as: multi-tenant school operations back office, admin/guru/parent

Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1 (unchanged by this work).

## Findings

| # | Rule | Pri | Finding |
|---|------|-----|---------|
| 1 | R-06, R-31 | MEDIUM | `--font-display` and `--font-mono` now both alias Montserrat, so the display and mono roles are inert. 56 `font-display` utilities, 10 `font-mono` utilities, and 6 CSS rules (`resources/css/app.css:139,183,329,356,472,925`) resolve to the same family as body text. Either write the reason these roles exist, or collapse the aliases and delete the dead classes. |
| 2 | R-06, R-20, R-37 | MEDIUM | Montserrat is a high-availability geometric sans, close to the model's default pick for "clean sans". The previous Inter + Fraunces pairing is what carried the editorial identity; with one family, headings separate only by size and weight. This was an explicit owner instruction, recorded here as a deliberate override (R-37), not an agent default. |
| 3 | R-06 | LOW | Montserrat is a wide face with large x-height. The 11px uppercase labels at `0.10em`-`0.18em` tracking (`.eyebrow` app.css:167, `.label` app.css:782, `.metric__label` app.css:349) will read loose. Tighten tracking to `0.04em`-`0.08em` or raise the size to 12px. |
| 4 | R-35 | LOW | Only verified at 1280px wide. No mobile pass was run at 390px. |
| 5 | C-4 | LOW | The standalone print report `students/rapor.blade.php` has no `preconnect` for `fonts.bunny.net` (the other two pages do). Pre-existing, not caused by this change. |
